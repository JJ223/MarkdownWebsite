## IT Support Technician — European Parliament Election
**Portuguese Government** · _June 2024_ · Portugal

Provided IT support for polling stations during the European Parliament elections, marking the first use of dematerialised electoral registers in Portugal. Trained polling station staff on the new digital systems, tested IT equipment and software prior to election day, and offered technical assistance throughout the process. Ensured a smooth transition to the new technology, enhancing the efficiency and reliability of the voting process.
